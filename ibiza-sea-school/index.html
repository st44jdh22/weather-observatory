Ibiza Sea School Weather · V5.39 · Forecast guidance, not operational clearance. Open-Meteo weather is primary; MET Norway Locationforecast is the automatic fallback. Marine wave data remains a separate Open-Meteo feed. These are numerical forecasts, not physical onboard observations. Pressure history is historical model-derived data. Offshore marine grid data is not a harbour or berth measurement. Official CAP alerts are retrieved automatically; local area-code coverage is still under validation. Check official AEMET current bulletins and local conditions. Do not use this app for navigation or safety-critical decisions alone. Weather/map content and availability are controlled by their providers.
