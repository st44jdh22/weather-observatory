Command, accountability & reporting

Captain · confirms weather and warnings, independently assesses conditions and makes all operational departure, diversion and cancellation decisions.

Marine Operations / IFO · reviews the existing handover and operational evidence trail, coordinates exceptions and escalations; does not replace the captain's judgement.

Future authenticated records · subject to Joe's approval and integration with the existing ODIN / Roger architecture. No duplicate master records.

A morning review must be repeated if conditions, warnings, route or operating plan materially change. This dashboard is supplementary; consult official forecasts and onboard instruments.

ARA Weather App · V5.13 · Forecast guidance, not operational clearance. Open-Meteo weather and marine data are numerical models, not physical onboard observations. Pressure history is historical model-derived data. Offshore marine grid data is not a harbour or berth measurement. Official CAP alerts are retrieved automatically; local area-code coverage is still under validation. Check official AEMET current bulletins and local conditions. Do not use this app for navigation or safety-critical decisions alone. Weather/map content and availability are controlled by their providers.
