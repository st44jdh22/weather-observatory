How to interpret this

Agreement is not probability

The headline measures how many pairs of models forecast wind speeds within 5 kn and wind directions within 45°. It is not an 80% likelihood of accurate weather.

Flag disagreement, don't average it away

Opposing model directions and divergent wind strengths should trigger manual review. Near-calm wind directions have limited meaning.

Official warnings remain primary

The final Weather Desk will present AEMET official bulletins and notices independently, without replacing them with model consensus.

Source and permissions

Live Windguru data will be added only through a private server-side API after the beta response format, licence scope and secret storage are verified. Required attribution will be preserved.
